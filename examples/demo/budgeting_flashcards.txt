Fixed costs	Same every month: rent, loan payments, insurance premiums.	ch1_ch1-s1
Variable costs	Change with your choices: groceries, fuel, entertainment.	ch1_ch1-s1
Irregular costs	Arrive a few times a year: repairs, annual subscriptions, gifts. Divide the yearly total by twelve and set it aside monthly.	ch1_ch1-s1
Surplus	What is left after all three; negative means the plan spends more than it earns.	ch1_ch1-s1
Needs	What you must pay to live and work.	ch1_ch1-s2
Wants	What you choose.	ch1_ch1-s2
Savings	Emergency fund, retirement, goals, extra debt payments.	ch1_ch1-s2
What is the surplus?	Income left after fixed, variable and irregular costs; negative means the plan overspends.	ch1_ch1-s4_selfcheck
What is zero-based budgeting?	Every unit of income gets a job so income minus planned spending equals zero; savings count as a job.	ch1_ch1-s4_selfcheck
